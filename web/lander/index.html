A CAD kernel for

popkern is the geometry engine that sits underneath CAD: the part that knows what a solid is, and how to cut it, join it, and round it off. It's open source, free forever, and aiming to be the best kernel there is. It isn't there yet, so come help get it there!

Made with popkern

Every one of these is an example in popkern live, rendered there: open one and the same script builds the same model in your browser. Each had to serve watertight to ship. The colors are the page's own views: a part's own finish, one color per part, or zebra stripes that show how smoothly surfaces meet.

Parts are scripts

Every popkern part is a short Rhai script. Rhai is a small, friendly scripting language, and the same script builds the same part in your browser or on your own machine.

Bigger parts grow as projects: a folder of numbered migrations, each picking up where the last one left off, much like database migrations. A later step can reach back and change an earlier feature, and the history scrubber replays the part one step at a time. A project is just a folder of text files, so it lives happily in git, and popkern build turns it into a .pkn document or a STEP file from the command line.

boss_plate/migrations 
// migrations/001_plate.rhai
let m = model();
m.profile(xy()).rect(2.4, 1.6)
    .sketch("plate_sketch").extrude(blind(0.4));
m

// migrations/002_boss.rhai
let m = previous();
let boss = m.tool(|m| m.profile(xy_at(0.2))
    .circle(0.35).sketch("boss_sketch").extrude(blind(0.8)));
m.active().union(boss);
m

// migrations/003_rounds.rhai
let m = previous();
m.active().fillet(edges().between("cylinder", "plane"),
    0.08, #{ label: "rounds" });
m

What works today

Quite a lot, actually! Booleans, fillets and chamfers (with properly smooth corners), sweeps, revolves and lofts, shelling, sketches with constraints, STEP and IGES in and out, and a full feature tree with undo. You build parts by writing short scripts, in your browser or on your own machine.

It's also early, so plenty doesn't work yet. The launch post is honest about what's missing.

Agent-built, test-verified

popkern is written by AI agents, working from published research under human direction. Every change has to pass thousands of automated tests and the kernel's own validation before it lands, and results are cross-checked against OpenCASCADE wherever the two can be compared. When an operation can't produce a valid solid, it declines and says why, rather than handing back something broken.

What you make is yours

The kernel is AGPL-3.0, so improvements to it stay open. An exception covers everything you make with it: your scripts, your models, your STEP files. Those are yours, full stop.

Come and break it.

If you've got a part popkern can't build, open an issue and it becomes a test case. That's the most useful thing anyone can do right now!
