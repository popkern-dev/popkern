Support popkern

Free forever, but not free to make.

popkern is free and always will be. If it's useful to you, or you just want a great open CAD kernel to exist in the world, chipping in really does help.

Building popkern costs real money every month, mostly for the AI agents that write the code, plus a lot of my time. Every bit of support goes straight back into the kernel.

Make a one-off donation

Can't chip in? No worries at all!

None of this has to be money. These help just as much:

  • Star the repo. It helps more people find popkern.
  • Use it. Build a part, then tell people it exists.
  • Break it. If popkern can't build your part, open an issue and it becomes a test case.
  • Pass it on. To anyone who makes things.

Thank you, truly. ♥
