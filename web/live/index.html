            popkern live
            
    
            
      
            A code-first CAD workbench on the popkern B-rep kernel: write a model script, build it, inspect the result, export STEP.
            
      
            Everything runs in this tab: your script and your model never leave the browser, nothing is uploaded, and no analytics are loaded.
